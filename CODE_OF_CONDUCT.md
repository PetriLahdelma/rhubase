# Code of Conduct

RhuBase aims to be a careful, welcoming community for people working across engineering, design, accessibility, research, and product disciplines.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and code without attacking people.
- Make room for different experience levels and communication styles.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property.
- Do not pressure anyone to share proprietary repositories or customer data.
- Correct mistakes openly, especially when a claim exceeds the evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person’s private information are not accepted in project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, project discussions, and public spaces where someone is representing RhuBase.

## Reporting and enforcement

For public, non-sensitive moderation concerns, open an issue using the repository’s community or conduct template when available. For urgent threats, abuse, or private safety concerns, use GitHub’s platform reporting tools rather than publishing sensitive details in the repository.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people.

No response-time commitment is promised while the maintainer community is being established.
